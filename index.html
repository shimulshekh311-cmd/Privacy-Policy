Privacy Policy for Bangla Typing Master (বাংলা টাইপিং মাস্টার)

Effective Date: November 2026

Bangla Typing Master ("we", "our", or "the app") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Bangla Typing Master is an educational typing practice application. We design our app to respect your privacy:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, phone number, address, or national ID.
  • Typing Data: Your typed text, practice records, and test scores are processed locally on your device to calculate typing speed (WPM) and accuracy. They are never sent to any external server.
  • Authentication: If you choose to sign in with Google, it is only used to save your practice history securely. We do not access your contacts, emails, or personal files.

2. Device Permissions

The app only requests standard permissions strictly required for its core features:

  • Internet Access: Used for optional cloud backup of test scores and displaying non-intrusive educational advertisements.
  • Vibration: Used to provide subtle haptic feedback when typing on mobile soft keyboards.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may use reputable third-party services that collect anonymized information used for standard app operations:

  • Google Play Services / Firebase Authentication
  • Google AdMob (for non-personalized educational ads)

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for students, job seekers, and learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
